01 Campaign poster · 3-hour turnaround

Haze Collection

Built in three hours for a clothing drop launching the following day. The layered type, electric color, and hard-edged collage capture the brand’s energy while maintaining enough contrast to make the date and collection name land instantly.

  • Rapid concepting
  • Photo compositing
  • Typography
02 Community campaign · Print and email

Paws & Whiskers Adoption Day

A fast-turnaround event poster built for both neighborhood posting and a rescue-center newsletter. Friendly color, expressive scale, and a clear information hierarchy help the event feel welcoming without losing the practical details.

  • Event design
  • Photo compositing
  • Information hierarchy
03 Book cover · Self-initiated

How to Win

A self-directed experiment in geometric storytelling. Charts, peaks, a staircase, and a distant flag turn a familiar success metaphor into a structured cover system with a deliberately restrained blue palette.

  • Cover design
  • Vector illustration
  • Visual metaphor
04 Identity · Large-format application

Safe Harbor Sealife Aquarium

A scalable identity created for murals, large posters, and environmental graphics. The harbor cross, starfish, and local marine life communicate an aquarium centered on healing, revitalization, and the study of nearby oceans.

  • Logo design
  • Vector illustration
  • Environmental scale
05 Wear OS · Product design

Guilloche Complication

A functional Wear OS face inspired by the engine-turned dials of eighteenth-century watchmaking. The project moved from paper studies to an Illustrator wireframe, Photoshop texture and lighting, Watch Face Studio engineering, and final Play Store campaign art.

  • Illustrator
  • Photoshop
  • Watch Face Studio
06 Wear OS · Interface design

Graphic World Timer

A functional watch face designed to make world time readable at a glance. The gradient horizon gives an immediate sense of day or night in each named city, while the ring structure keeps a dense set of information legible.

  • Information design
  • Illustrator
  • Watch Face Studio
07 Poster series · Personal work

Personal Wall Posters

Three studies in visual language: a Bauhaus exhibition homage, a detail-led celebration of the Mercedes-Benz CLK GTR, and a Product Sans specimen directed by the clarity and playfulness of Google’s interface design.

  • Typography
  • Art direction
  • Editorial layout
08 Print · Fabrication

Display Firearm Posters

A hybrid print-and-object system made to explain a firearm’s design while physically displaying it. Each custom poster was plotted, mounted to painted MDF, and fitted with purpose-placed hardware so the object and its visual story could live together.

  • Layout
  • Large-format print
  • Fabrication
09 Industrial concepts · In progress

Prototypes in Progress

An under-monitor MagSafe mount that holds an iPhone horizontally while charging, paired with a cast-brass recreation of the iconic Loïe Fuller lamp developed through a 3D-printed mold.

  • 3D printing
  • Product thinking
  • Metal casting
