About William

Curious by nature.
Exacting by choice.

18

I’m William Shepelev, a multidisciplinary designer creating digital assets since 2018.

I grew up in the Portland metro area and am now based in Ormond Beach, Florida. Design plays into how I think every day: I like finding the structure inside an idea, then giving it enough character to be remembered.

My work moves between graphic design, advertising, identity, functional Wear OS faces, 3D concepts, and physical fabrication. I’m equally interested in the first sketch and the last detail — and in learning whatever the project needs between those points.

Outside the studio, I enjoy trying new things and exploring the city around me. That curiosity often comes back into the work as a new reference, material, or way of solving a problem.
